Dataset created during the PestNu project (H2020, under Grant Agreement no. 101037128). The dataset consists of 225 4k (3840x2160) images of ~5900 annotations across 2 classes:
* Aphids (ap): in total 3,473 annotated insects
* Whiteflies (wh): in total 2,431 annotated insects
